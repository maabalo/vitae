Save your resume as PDF

Your browser's print window makes the PDF. Set it up like this:

  1. Destination (or Printer): choose Save as PDF.
  2. Paper size: A4, to match your design.
  3. Headers and footers: turn off (under "More settings").
  4. Background graphics: turn on to keep the accent colors.
  5. Click Save.

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share → Print, then pinch out on the preview and share it as a PDF.

# readme

What is Vitae?

Vitae builds a clean, professional resume (CV). Fill in your details, pick a design, and save it as a PDF that's ready to send. Everything stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

01 Quick start

  1. Not sure where to begin? Open data → load example to see a finished resume.
  2. Fill in the form from top to bottom. The preview updates as you type.
  3. Use add job, add project, and the others to add entries. Use the arrows to reorder them.
  4. Choose a template, paper size and accent color in 01 design.
  5. Click save PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the print window.

On a phone, switch between edit and preview with the tabs at the top.

02 Writing tips

  • One page is best for under 10 years of experience. The page counter in the preview helps.
  • Start each highlight with an action verb: built, led, reduced, improved.
  • Add numbers wherever you can: cut load time by 48%, managed 5 people.
  • Put your most recent job first.
  • The PDF holds real text, not a picture, so applicant tracking systems (ATS) can read it.

03 Fields

Highlights / details
Each line becomes one bullet point.
Start / end
Free text, such as Jan 2022 or 2019. Tick "currently" to show Present.
Skills
One group per line. Tools: Git, Figma shows "Tools" in bold.
Empty fields
Empty fields and sections are left out of the resume automatically.

04 Your data

  • Your resume is saved automatically in this browser. The status next to the tabs shows when.
  • Use data → export .json to make a backup or move your resume to another device, and import .json to load it back.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes the saved resume, so keep a backup.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Vitae works offline once the page has loaded.
